# helpout: old forms and the H-1 layout at every level

## Why
Thing-first rides in mankit v4 (owner, 19 Nov). Every command has exactly one full form: no shortcuts. A renamed command keeps its old form for one release.

## Changes
- `helpout.Old(cmd, words...)` marks a command's old forms. `Execute` makes each a hidden command: never listed, runs its full form with the rest of the line, and says the new name on stderr, so stdout stays the same.
- `<app> <thing> help`, `<app> <thing> --help` and `<app> help <thing>` show the thing's layout at every level. No Shortcuts block.
- `<app> help -o json` lists commands and flags as data, with old forms marked old (waits on a contracts schema, see the PR).
- The old forms and helptest go at mankit's next major (card 20261117-0f8b).
