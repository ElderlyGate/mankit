# mankit's next major deletes the old forms and helptest

## Why
Thing-first rides in v4.0.0 (owner, 19 Nov); renamed commands keep their old forms for one release. Once every app has dropped its old forms, the next major deletes them (owner: "we need to delete this").

## What to delete
- The files `helpout/old.go`, `helpout/old_test.go` and the package `helpout/helptest` (with every app's helptest.Check test).
- In `helpout/helpout.go`, `Execute`:
  - the `addOld(root)` call and its error return;
  - the block after `root.ExecuteContextC(ctx)` that starts `// An old form says its new name on stderr` (`var old *rerun` … the second `ExecuteContextC`).
- In `helpout/helpout.go`, `addThingHelp`: the `&& !isOld(sub)` check.
- In `helpout/data.go`:
  - in `helpData`, `Old: OldForms(root)` becomes `Old: []OldForm{}`, and the `d.Old == nil` guard goes;
  - in `answerHelp`, the line `data.Old = oldUnder(data.Old, words(root, topic))`.
- Every `helpout.Old(…)` and `helpout.OldArgs(…)` mark in mankit and the apps.
- serve's `confirm=yes` and `confirm=remember` (`serve.ConfirmYes`, `serve.ConfirmRemember` and their cases in the gate), once every page and client sends the Question's id: over HTTP a page from elsewhere could send them, as the cookie goes along by itself and another port on this machine counts as the same site. The CLI keeps `--yes`.
- README: the section "Old forms of renamed commands".

## What stays
- `HelpData.Old` and the type `OldForm` stay, always an empty list: contracts mankit/v1 `Help` requires `old`. The field leaves the schema only at mankit/v2 of contracts.

## Before the removal
- A hand grep across the repos and skills for old forms, in that PR's review.

## Waits on
- Every app's board card for dropping its old forms (depends_on).
