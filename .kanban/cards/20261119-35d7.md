# Flaky: TestChangesHandTheTurnOnQuickly under parallel load

## Why
`store/internal/queue/queue_test.go:701` checks that 20 changes of 1 ms finish in under one second. Alone it passes in about 0.45 s. In a full `go test ./...` run, with many packages testing in parallel, it once took 1.86 s and failed. The wall-clock bound measures the machine's load, not the queue's hand-over.

## Changes
- Make the check measure the hand-over itself, not the whole run's wall time: for example, the time between one change ending and the next starting, or a bound scaled to a measured baseline. A wider fixed bound would only move the flake.
- Keep what it guards: a turn passes on quickly, never waiting the long pause (`lastPause`) a ticket further back waits; the median hand-over stays under half of it. It is not held to `firstInLine` (20 ms) itself: with every CPU busy the median gap reached 22 ms.

## Done when
- [ ] The test passes in repeated full parallel runs (`go test -count=5 ./...`) and still fails if the hand-over waits `lastPause` every time.
