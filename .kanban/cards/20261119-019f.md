# features: every feature has a tier, free or enterprise

## Why
Step S4 of the license epic (control-room card KBN-20261106-e99b). ADR-L2: each feature has a tier, and only an enterprise feature can be locked. The tier's meaning lives in contracts featureState.tier (contracts#45).

## Changes
- `features.Feature` gains `Tier` (`features.Free`, `features.Enterprise`; the zero value is free), answered as `tier`, left out when free (contracts#45: absent means free).
- `features.License` is the `by` word of the license closer; a closer by license never closes a free feature (ADR-L2).
- A list holding a tier it does not know is refused at Attach and States, like the other wiring mistakes.

## Waits on
Nothing. The license package (card for feature license-check) builds on it.
