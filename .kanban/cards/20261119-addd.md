# mankit's own writers through store scopes, one route

## Why
ADR-S22: every app keeps what it writes through the one storage port, and mankit is no exception (owner, 19 Nov: one route, no ADR change). SP4 (card 20261119-f862) left mankit's own writers on the safety core `internal/disk`, below the port, taking no turn. This is step PR2 of the v4 rework; PR1 (card 20261119-7f72) breaks the import loop that kept them there.

## Changes
- settings, ask, prefs, usercmd, palette, split, `store.Migrate` and `store.CopyOnce` write through a `store.Config` or `store.Data` scope, by key, in a turn; the path-taking APIs open the scope the path lies in and refuse a path outside every app's own folders.
- serve's servers records and token go through `store.Data`; the token's flock goes, the turn does its work.
- `internal/disk` moves under the store, so nothing else can write past the port (Go's internal rule).
- A `config.yaml` that is a link is refused, never followed and never replaced (ADR-S22: nothing follows a link out; owner, 19 Nov). Reading it is unchanged.
- The detached server's log waits for the owner's answer (OpenTelemetry log format, locally); serve/process.go is not touched until then.
