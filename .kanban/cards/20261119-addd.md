# split, usercmd and palette write through store scopes

## Why
SP4 (card 20261119-f862) moved them onto the inner safety core `internal/disk`, because their exported APIs take a file path, not a scope. Above the port, they can keep what they write through `store.Config` like any app (ADR-S22).

## Changes
- split's width, palette's favorites and usercmd's `config.yaml` (Init, Migrate) go through a `store.Config` scope, by key.
- The path-taking APIs stay until the next major, or give way to scope-taking ones beside them; nothing exported is removed in a minor.
- `config.yaml` stays a dotfiles link where a person made it one (owner, 19 Nov): how a scope keeps that is part of this card.
