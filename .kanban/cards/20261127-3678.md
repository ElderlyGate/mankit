# store.CopyOnce names the app after the target's last folder

- Found by kanban-app (card ddd4, stopgap there): `CopyOnce(from, to)` takes the app from `home.ID(filepath.Base(to))` (`ownFolder`). Copying `<data>/kanban-app/hooks` runs as app `hooks` and leaves `<data>/elderlygate/hooks/.store/<host>` beside the product's own folder (same for events, servers).
- ADR-S22: a journal lives in the app's own data folder.
- Wanted: CopyOnce takes an App or a Scope.
