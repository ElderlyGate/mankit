# change queue (mankit serve step 3)

Step 3 of epic 20261111-0ff3. One pull request, one release. Decisions: ADR-S14, S15, S16, S19; block CLI-API in the constitution design.

- Package `queue`: every change to one served thing waits its turn in one queue in `<served folder>/<app folder>/.queue/` (0700). Tickets, not commands: each process runs its own change; nothing in the folder is executed. One at a time across processes; a change is done once written and read back.
- Stale or unreadable tickets move to `.queue/bad/`, are said and skipped; waiting times out. Errors carry codes.
- What `<app> queue` needs: the list of tickets in order and the bad ones, in the contracts shape. No app's CLI here.
- Tickets and the list validate against contracts `mankit/v1` (ElderlyGate/contracts#4).

Not here: telemetry, connect; apps switching to the queue.
