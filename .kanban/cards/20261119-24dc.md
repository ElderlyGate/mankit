# Next major: path writes take a context, so a nested write finds its change

## Why
`store.EditFile` (mankit#59) joins a change that holds its folder's turn by the goroutine running that change's fn, read from the runtime's stack, since Go names no goroutine. It works, but a goroutine fn starts is not known as the change's, and the id comes from a stack trace (review of mankit#59).

## Changes
- `store.EditFile`, `settings.Set`, `ask.Set` and the other path writers take a `context.Context`; a change's fn hands its Tx on in the context, and a nested write joins the change it finds there.
- The goroutine registry goes.
- A breaking change of exported signatures: the next major of mankit.
