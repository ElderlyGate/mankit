# build: stamp the product line

A sold product's release build stamps the product line whose tags hold its own version, and `--version` prints the line first, like `Designer 1 (v10.3.2)` (org rules V-5, V-10). The lines live in one file of the org, `product-lines.yaml`, read at build time only, never by a running app.

Work: a small reader next to the version stamp in `build`: given the file, the product and the build's tag, it gives the line (none for a side product or a dev build), and `--version` shows it. Where the public name ("Designer") comes from: the org's product list.
