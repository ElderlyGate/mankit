# mankit v3: Problems and envelope v2

Step P part 3b (constitution card KBN-20261113-347c): mankit serve writes Problems (problem/v1) in envelope/v2, and the version answer (version/v1).

## Changes
- Module path `/v3`.
- `App.Contracts` is a list of `Contract{Version, Envelope}`. Envelope is v1 (the default) or v2, and never goes back. A contract on envelope/v1 keeps its bytes: `ran`, error/v1 codes, and `supported`.
- Types and constructors: `Problem`, `Place`, `Source`, and `Fail(type, reason, detail)`, with Type and Reason constants.
- Words: serve, queue, events and features use the owner's mapping, with failed or refused plus the old word.
- `GET /api/version` answers version/v1 for every contract and never refuses one.
- A guard refusal is answered in the contract the reader asked for, when the app serves it.
- `servers` reads both envelopes.
- Tests:
  - golden v1 bytes from v2.0.0;
  - every answer kind validated against contracts v0.15.0.
